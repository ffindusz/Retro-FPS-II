# Fortress environment

The default 0.4 presentation uses [bundled CC0 photographic stone](photographic/README.md) with 1K color, roughness and OpenGL normal maps. The original 0.3 images below remain preserved as authoring assets and historical evidence; they are no longer the default world materials.

## Original 0.3 procedural surfaces

Six original 512×512 RGB PNG images are authored offline by `tools/generate_fortress_assets.gd`, not downloaded or generated by a paid service. Each family has albedo and roughness maps:

| Family | Pattern in one tile | Seed | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| wall | 128×64px staggered blocks | 17 | Rough block-stone walls |
| floor | 256×256px staggered slabs | 29 | Flagstone floors |
| trim | 256×128px staggered blocks | 43 | Worn stone trim/pillars |

Periodic low-frequency variation, deterministic fine grain, seeded per-block colors, dark mortar and edge wear create the surfaces. The authoring script asserts staggered boundary wrapping. Mipmaps are enabled; StandardMaterial3D uses repeat sampling and world-aligned triplanar coordinates at `uv1_scale = (0.5, 0.5, 0.5)`, approximately two meters per image tile. Colors vary slightly by entrance/courtyard/crypt through shared cached materials. Iron/brass accents use shared solid-color rough metallic materials, not additional images.

Source image sizes/material mappings are tested; human seam/scale/readability approval remains separate. No Meshy source files, model normalization, actor materials or 1024px model texture limits are modified. These original procedural assets need no third-party texture attribution; the owner chooses the project's overall distribution license.

## Original 0.4.1 moonlit sky

`sky/moonlit_fortress.png` is one original 1024x512 RGB panorama, authored offline with spherical smooth-simplex clouds, 160 seeded star stamps and a softly clouded moon. The fixed seed is 18419. The moon follows the existing directional light's (-55, -30, 0) degree rotation; longitude is mapped clockwise from -Z for Godot's PanoramaSkyMaterial. Edge columns are identical, cloud sampling is spherical and star stamps wrap without touching the poles.

```powershell
& .\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --path . --script tools/generate_fortress_sky.gd
& .\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --path . --editor --import
```

[Provenance](sky/provenance.json) records the generator, engine, parameters and SHA-256. Import preserves RGB with mipmaps and a 1024px size cap. Each run owns its Sky/PanoramaSkyMaterial and shares the bundled image; nothing is generated or downloaded during play. Explicit color ambient and disabled sky reflections prevent it changing world/model lighting. The weapon viewport stays isolated, and atmosphere-off retains the flat background. No external art, paid generation or third-party attribution is involved; the owner chooses the project's distribution license. The user approved the sky's general appearance; a detailed manual seam review remains unreported, separate from the passing technical seam and alignment checks.
